# Coach Cass Brand Identity

## Colors
- Primary: #ff7095 (vibrant pink)
- Background: #171117 (dark charcoal/nearly black)
- Surface/Panel: #2f282e (dark muted purple/grey)
- Text/On-Surface: #ecdfe8 (off-white/light lavender)
- Accent: #e9c349 (gold)

## Typography
- Headlines: Noto Serif (italic for emphasis)
- Body: Manrope

## Design Rules
- Remove all stock photography of people. No AI-generated women or placeholder portraits.
- Use glass-panel blur effects on input containers.
- Generous whitespace, elegant luxury feel.
